**Coming from 1.0.0?** Download and install this version by hand once (below). From now on the app checks for new versions itself and installs them for you: Profile → App updates.

**Find the right bus by its name**
- Route page: *Which bus goes this way* lists the next departures in the direction shown, with the names of the buses that run them (Chitra, BRTC-05 …), the time and how long until they leave.
- Stop page: *Which bus to take here* gives the next bus of every route that picks up there.
- Predicted buses are named by their buses, not just the route number.

**Home-screen widget**
- Your next buses on the home screen without opening the app, with the bus names and a countdown that keeps ticking.
- Shows the last bus of the day, then *No more buses today* with tomorrow's first.
- Tap a bus to open its route in the app. Each widget can show different buses: long-press it and choose Settings, or set it up in Profile → Home-screen widget.

**Near you**
- The three nearest stops, how far each is on foot and the next bus there. Your position stays on your phone.
- Arrival alerts: tap the bell on a bus heading for your stop to be told when it is a few minutes away.
- The location button follows you, then turns the map the way you face; your dot shows your direction.

**Map**
- Buses on top of each other share one marker with a count; tap it to pick one.
- Arrows show which way each route runs, and a picked bus shows the part it has already covered.
- Following a bus shows where it goes next and how fresh its position is.
- Works on tablets and with the phone on its side.

**Also**
- About page with the developer and source code links.
- Still early access, signed with the same development key as 1.0.0, so it installs over 1.0.0 without uninstalling.
