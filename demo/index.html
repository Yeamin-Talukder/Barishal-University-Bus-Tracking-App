How this demo works

  1. The driver's phone sends a GPS point every 5 seconds.
  2. The server snaps it onto the route line, which removes GPS jitter.
  3. The server works out the next stop, speed and arrival times.
  4. The app glides the bus along the road until the next point arrives.

Here, steps 1–3 run inside this page with two simulated buses. The road, stops and map are real (OpenStreetMap). In your app, the points come from the drivers' phones through your server.
